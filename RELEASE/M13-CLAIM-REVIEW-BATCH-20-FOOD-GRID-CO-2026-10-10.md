## Method
Reviewed the 15 Claim statements and their linked Evidence Use records from the frozen M13 worksheet. Checked current public institutional guidance from FDA/USDA FSIS, CDC/CPSC, U.S. EIA and U.S. Department of Energy. D (explanatory depth), E (claim-specific evidence fit) and B (boundary discipline) are scored independently from 0–3. Scores represent desk review, not a complete domain audit or operational certification.

## Findings
1. **Confirmed Evidence Use documentation debt:** the three `food-safety-basics` Claims A/B/C all use the same generic evidence description. Their broad source is relevant, but the record-level description does not expose the specific support. This is an evidence-description defect, not evidence that the Claims are false.
2. **Good evidence separation:** the CDC cooking-temperature Claim explicitly avoids being misrepresented as independent corroboration for the FDA-backed claims.
3. **Useful regional boundary:** grid claims avoid asserting that every region has identical structure. DOE/EIA material is U.S.-specific and should be labelled as such where institutional framing matters.
4. **High-consequence CO guidance is comparatively strong:** emergency response, indoor-use prohibition and maintenance claims have specific institutional support. Alarm placement should retain local-code/manufacturer boundaries.
5. No content records were changed. These 15 Claims are part of the fixed 1,494-Claim denominator. This batch does not close food safety, electrical/grid safety, CO safety, the high-consequence pass, or M13.
