## 4. Cross-record, source and rights findings

1. **Software architecture:** the registered SEI source is well matched to Claims A and B. Claim C overstates the boundary between architecture and implementation; architecture views can include data, interfaces and algorithmic choices where they are architecturally significant. Clarify rather than categorically exclude these elements.
2. **Software engineering source mismatch:** `SRC-SOFTWARE_ENGINEERING_BASICS` uses a descriptive statement as `source_identity` and points to the NIST ITL home page. That page is a laboratory landing page, not a software engineering life-cycle source.
3. **Soil source access/precision:** the USDA NRCS topic URI is broad and was not independently retrievable in this pass. Exact “What is Soil?” and “Soil Health Assessment” pages are better candidates for composition and functional effects.
4. **ISO rights hold:** `SRC-STANDARDS_AND_CERTIFICATION_BASICS` points to ISO “Standards.” The page's own terms explicitly prohibit AI/ML use, including prompting/generation, outside ISO Open Data absent permission. All three Claims are therefore on RIGHTS HOLD for this AI-assisted workflow. Do not use or reproduce ISO material in the evidence workflow without written permission. Replace with an eligible standards-policy or accreditation source, not a copied ISO definition.
5. **Statistics inference:** the NIST/SEMATECH handbook is relevant, but the root URI is not a locator. Use the glossary, populations/sampling, model assumptions, quantitative techniques and confidence-interval sections as exact locators.
6. **Infrastructure:** the OECD Infrastructure page is a navigation hub. The claims need specific infrastructure planning, asset lifecycle, risk, service capacity and demand sources; the hub alone is insufficient.
7. **Sustainable development:** the UN SDG portal supports the three dimensions and interdependence at a high level. Paragraph 55 of A/RES/70/1 is the strongest exact source for the “integrated and indivisible” proposition.
8. **ILO reuse:** the ILO Social Protection URL is the same source already used in the social-protection and social-insurance slices. It is one institutional source, not multiple independent corroborations. The present Claim requires an explicit measurement framework for coverage, adequacy and accessibility.
9. **Evidence Use quality:** all 20 Evidence Use descriptions remain generic and lack exact passages. Even where the source is a strong conceptual match, the evidence records are not yet pinpoint-complete.
10. All 20 Claims currently have `publication_status=published` and `completion_status=complete`; these fields do not override the M13 `UNASSESSED` status or constitute a new release decision.
