## Passage checked

The current Source record points to NIST, *Fire Protection and Life Safety for Design and Construction Program*: https://www.nist.gov/oshe/safety-programs/fire-protection-and-life-safety-design-and-construction-program. Under the applicability section, NIST lists fire-alarm system components; fire-rated construction and smoke-control features; and means-of-egress components, including exit access, travel paths, exit doors, stairs, ramps, exit signs and emergency lighting. These categories directly support the Claim's general inventory of detection/alarm systems, fire-rated/compartmentation features, and organized exit routes.

Checked on: 2026-10-16. The official page was accessible. Scope limitation: the program sets requirements for NIST's own design and construction program; it does not by itself establish the code requirements for every building or jurisdiction.
