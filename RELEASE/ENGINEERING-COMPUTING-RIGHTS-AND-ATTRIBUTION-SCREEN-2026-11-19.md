## Purpose and limits

This is a source-specific rights and attribution screen for the eight remediated engineering/computing slices. It records publicly available licence/permission information for the exact source families and editions currently linked by the canonical Source records.

It is **not legal advice, a legal opinion, a permission grant, or final rights clearance**. Public availability is not treated as public domain. The review distinguishes:
- factual paraphrase and bibliographic citation currently present in the canonical records;
- copying, excerpting, translating, adapting, or redistributing expressive source material;
- source-specific rights from the still-unselected licence and distribution model for project-authored Encyclopedia content.

The current canonical records contain independently worded factual propositions and source locators. The remediation did not copy source paragraphs, code, figures, tables, diagrams, logos, or images into the records. That fact reduces the amount of third-party expression currently present, but does not by itself close every legal question.
