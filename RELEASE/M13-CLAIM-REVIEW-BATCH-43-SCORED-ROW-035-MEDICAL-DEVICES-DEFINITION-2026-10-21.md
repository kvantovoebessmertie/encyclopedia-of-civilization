## Passage checked

Canonical Source: World Health Organization (WHO), “Medical Devices,” https://www.who.int/health-topics/medical-devices.

The WHO page defines a medical device as an instrument, apparatus, implement, machine, appliance, implant, in-vitro reagent, software, material or related article intended by the manufacturer for a medical purpose, alone or in combination. The same page describes devices used in varied settings for prevention and screening, diagnosis, monitoring, treatment and assistance, and states that there are an estimated two million different kinds on the world market. This supports the Claim's general definition and the statement that the category spans a broad range.

The Claim is a compressed summary, not a complete regulatory definition. “Medical purposes” should be understood in the sense of the manufacturer's intended medical purpose; exact legal definitions and classification may vary by applicable regulatory framework. The canonical Evidence Use record points to the correct WHO topic page but does not retain an exact passage or section pinpoint.
