## Method and limits

For each candidate, the current Claim JSON, primary Evidence Use JSON, and referenced Source JSON were inspected. The pass checked exact filename/internal `record_id`, Claim target, Source target, versions, evidence role, description specificity, source identity and stored URI. It is a source-fit triage, not a full external-source passage audit. A resolvable URI and `supports` role do not prove that a source directly supports the proposition. No D/E/B score was assigned, and no Claim, Evidence Use, Source, or Relation JSON was edited.
