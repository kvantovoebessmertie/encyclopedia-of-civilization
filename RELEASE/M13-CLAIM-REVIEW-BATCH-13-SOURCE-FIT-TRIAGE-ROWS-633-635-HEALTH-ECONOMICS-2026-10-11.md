## Source trail and public-source pinpoints

The Claims reference WHO *Health Economics*: https://www.who.int/health-topics/health-economics

Relevant sections of that page:
- **Overview**: technical and allocative efficiency; costing and budget impact; cost-effectiveness analysis; health technology assessment; benefit package design, priority setting and resource allocation.
- **Costing and Technical Efficiency**: resource-use assessment to determine feasibility, efficiency, affordability and sustainability of strategies.
- **Health and the Economy**: economic consequences of ill-health at household, firm, government and national levels.

For Claim C, the stored WHO health-economics page alone is not a clear pinpoint for all of financial protection and universal health coverage. WHO has more direct pages:
- Health financing: https://www.who.int/health-topics/health-financing/
- Financial protection: https://www.who.int/health-topics/universal-health-coverage/financial-protection
- Universal health coverage: https://www.who.int/health-topics/universal-health-coverage/

The three canonical Evidence Use records contain generic summaries and no exact section pinpoints. Claim C therefore needs either an explicit additional Source/Evidence Use chain or narrower wording, not an implied citation to sources that are not linked.

## 1. `CLM-HEALTH_ECONOMICS_BASICS-A` — provisional **D2 / E3 / B2**

**Claim:** “Экономика здравоохранения рассматривает распределение ресурсов, затраты, бюджетное воздействие и эффективность в системах здравоохранения.”

**Assessment:** This gives a useful map of the field but does not distinguish technical from allocative efficiency or explain evaluation methods (D2). WHO's Health Economics overview directly lists resource allocation, costing, budget impact, cost-effectiveness and both technical and allocative efficiency, so the core list is strongly supported (E3). The statement is appropriately general; retain the understanding that economic evaluation informs decisions and does not alone determine social or ethical priorities (B2).

**Disposition:** Retain provisionally. Add a pinpoint to the WHO overview and the costing/technical-efficiency section.

## 2. `CLM-HEALTH_ECONOMICS_BASICS-B` — provisional **D2 / E3 / B2**

**Claim:** “Оценка экономических последствий помогает анализировать выбор между альтернативами в условиях ограниченных ресурсов.”

**Assessment:** The claim describes the decision-support role but not incremental costs, outcomes, uncertainty or opportunity cost (D2). WHO directly describes prioritization among interventions and resource allocation under constraints, with feasibility, cost, budget impact and efficiency considerations. This supports the central proposition (E3). “Помогает анализировать” is appropriately cautious; do not imply economic evaluation mechanically selects a universally correct option without values, distributional effects or context (B2).

**Disposition:** Retain provisionally. Pinpoint the WHO overview's priority-setting/resource-allocation content and its costing/technical-efficiency section.

## 3. `CLM-HEALTH_ECONOMICS_BASICS-C` — provisional **D2 / E2 / B2**

**Claim:** “WHO связывает экономический анализ здравоохранения с вопросами эффективности, финансовой защиты и универсального охвата услугами.”

**Assessment:** This is a concise institutional summary but does not explain the distinct concepts or how they are measured (D2). WHO's Health Economics page directly supports efficiency; financial protection and UHC are more directly documented on WHO's separate health-financing/UHC pages. Those pages were identified in the review but are not represented by the canonical Source/Evidence Use chain, which currently links only the Health Economics page. Evidence fit to the current record chain is therefore partial (E2). The claim is appropriately attributed to WHO and does not assert that efficiency guarantees financial protection or universal coverage (B2).

**Disposition:** Keep provisional, not release-grade. Either add a versioned WHO Source record and Evidence Use for the financial-protection/UHC component, or narrow the statement to the concepts directly present on the current source page.
