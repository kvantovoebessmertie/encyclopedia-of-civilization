Do not infer risk from the list order. Route the next work in this order:
1. **Safety and consequence first:** electrical safety, emergency management, shelter, water security, hydropower, calibration, electronics and anatomy. Recheck the latest high-consequence review reports before opening new work; several candidates already appear in broad safety/health review files.
2. **Check report body before repeating research:** for each candidate, inspect whether the cited report actually reviewed the exact Claim version, exact EU and exact Source passage. Record the report section and disposition.
3. **Source/rights review:** confirm bibliographic identity, edition, exact passage and intended-use rights. OpenStax-derived material remains rights-sensitive; a public URL or a direct passage does not grant reuse permission.
4. **Canonical correction only after the exact record/version, pinpoint, rights state and regression are authorized.** Add a new version where required; preserve historical versions and score rows.
5. **Independent adjudication and Human View remain separate gates.** A desk review by the same operator cannot be recorded as independent review.

## 4. Machine-scan limitations

- The scan matches exact Claim-ID text in report bodies and classifies source-review reports by filename hints. It does not parse report sections or prove that a candidate's own Claim/EU/Source chain was reviewed.
- A filename match can be a false positive for substantive coverage; no filename match can be a false negative when a relevant review is embedded in a broader report.
- The artifact was generated at source revision `3fefb3fcba27da3f722f668d0f373ccb49811ca9`. The subsequent `98aa3b7b808b101b584d2e60d42e7186059bca59` change adds quarantine for conflicting worksheet mappings and a regression test; it does not change canonical content or report files. Re-run the coverage workflow after the next relevant code/report change and reconcile its source revision before relying on counts.
