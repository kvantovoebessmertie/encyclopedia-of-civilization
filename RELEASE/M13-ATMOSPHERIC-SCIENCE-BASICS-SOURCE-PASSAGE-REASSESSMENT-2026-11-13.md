## 2. Claim-level passage review

### Claim A — `CLM-ATMOSPHERIC_SCIENCE_BASICS-A@1`

Claim: “Атмосфера является газовой оболочкой Земли, в которой происходят метеорологические процессы.”

Direct official passages:
- NOAA, “Layers of the Atmosphere”, opening: describes the atmosphere as the envelope of gas surrounding Earth and says its layers are distinguished by thermal characteristics, chemical composition, movement and density.
  https://www.noaa.gov/jetstream/atmosphere/layers-of-atmosphere
- NASA, “What Is… Earth’s Atmosphere?”, “When we talk about the Earth's Atmosphere, what do we mean?”: describes layers of gas surrounding Earth; its troposphere section says Earth's weather occurs in this layer.
  https://www.nasa.gov/general/what-is-earths-atmosphere/

Assessment:
- Direct support for the gas-envelope definition and meteorological processes/weather occurring within the atmosphere.
- The statement is introductory and appropriately bounded.
- Proposed locators: NOAA “Layers of the Atmosphere”, opening paragraph; NASA “When we talk about the Earth's Atmosphere…” and “1. Troposphere”.
- Passage status: **DIRECT**.

### Claim B — `CLM-ATMOSPHERIC_SCIENCE_BASICS-B@1`

Claim: “Распределение температуры, давления и состава меняется с высотой и во времени.”

Direct official passages:
- NOAA, “Layers of the Atmosphere”: layers are distinguished by temperature, chemical composition, movement and density; it describes layer-specific temperature and composition features and the change in density/temperature with height.
- NOAA, “Air Pressure”: “as elevation increases” molecule count and density decrease, and air pressure decreases with height; the page also explains pressure changes due to temperature/density and daily and weather-system-related pressure fluctuations.
  https://www.noaa.gov/jetstream/atmosphere/air-pressure
- NASA, “What Is… Earth’s Atmosphere?”: identifies layers by temperature, chemical composition and density; describes temperature changes with altitude in the troposphere, stratosphere and mesosphere; explains that aerosols and anthropogenic/natural emissions change atmospheric composition.

Assessment:
- Direct support for temperature variation with altitude, pressure variation with altitude and time, and composition differences among layers and changes over time through emissions/aerosols.
- Precision note: the claim's phrase “распределение состава” is broad. It should distinguish vertical differences among layers from temporal changes in concentrations caused by sources/processes. Do not imply that every constituent varies in the same way or at the same rate.
- Proposed locators: NOAA “Layers of the Atmosphere” opening and layer descriptions; NOAA “Air Pressure”, passages on altitude, temperature/density and daily/weather-system pressure changes; NASA, “When we talk about…” and “Pollution/Air Quality”.
- Passage status: **DIRECT, with terminology precision note**.

### Claim C — `CLM-ATMOSPHERIC_SCIENCE_BASICS-C@1`

Claim: “Атмосферные процессы связаны с переносом энергии и вещества.”

Direct official passages:
- NASA, “What Is… Earth’s Atmosphere?”, “1. Troposphere”: describes temperature gradients driving motion in air and water; evaporation/transpiration, movement of water vapour through the troposphere, condensation, cloud movement and precipitation.
- NASA, “Greenhouse Gases”: explains that atmospheric gases absorb and re-emit outgoing longwave radiation and that incoming shortwave solar energy is reflected, scattered or absorbed.
- NASA, “Pollution/Air Quality”: describes aerosols entering the atmosphere from natural and anthropogenic sources and changing atmospheric composition.
  https://www.nasa.gov/general/what-is-earths-atmosphere/

Assessment:
- Direct support for matter transport through the water cycle and aerosols, and energy transfer through solar/terrestrial radiation and atmospheric absorption/re-emission.
- The Claim is broad but defensible as an introductory systems statement. A future explanation should distinguish radiative energy transfer, convection/advection and hydrological/material transport rather than imply one mechanism.
- Proposed locators: NASA “1. Troposphere”, “Greenhouse Gases”, and “Pollution/Air Quality”.
- Passage status: **DIRECT**.

## 4. Proposed editorial refinements (not canonical)

- A: current wording is acceptable as an introductory definition.
- B: consider “Температура, давление, плотность и состав атмосферы различаются по высоте; отдельные параметры также меняются во времени под влиянием нагрева, циркуляции, погоды и источников примесей.” This is more explicit about vertical and temporal variation and avoids suggesting uniform change across all constituents.
- C: keep the broad statement, but explain separately the transport of water vapour/aerosols and the transfer of radiative/thermal energy.
