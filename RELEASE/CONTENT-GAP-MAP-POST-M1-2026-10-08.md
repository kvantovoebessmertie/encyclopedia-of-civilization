## Audit method

This Gap Map is a fresh post-M1 prioritization against the current registry and current record structure. It does not reuse the historical pre-M1 candidate list as authority.

A targeted structural review of twenty representative older-profile slices showed a recurring 9-record pattern:
- 3 Claims
- 3 Evidence Use records
- 1 Context
- 1 Scope
- 1 Source

The pattern is structurally valid but creates an evidence-diversity and applicability asymmetry when compared with matured slices that carry an independent second evidence track.

Priority is therefore based on material value, consequence, and reuse—not on an artificial record quota.

### G2 — Applicability and interpretation boundaries

The second audit question is not simply whether another source exists. Each candidate must be checked for a real semantic boundary:
- air quality: pollutant/measurement context versus universal health conclusions;
- agriculture: system/context dependence and non-universal practice claims;
- anatomy: descriptive anatomy versus clinical interpretation;
- antimicrobial resistance: population/public-health information versus individual treatment advice;
- acid-base: model/context boundaries and hazardous-procedure non-inference;
- analytical chemistry: method/measurement context and uncertainty;
- algebra: mathematical model assumptions and interpretation;
- agrifood systems: system boundary and causal overclaiming;
- architecture: descriptive principles versus engineering/code requirements;
- ancient civilizations: evidence limits and temporal sequence versus causality.

### G3 — Human View / safety

Health, environmental, chemistry and engineering content must preserve limitations and must not be rendered as individualized medical, regulatory, or hazardous procedural advice.

Historical content must preserve uncertainty where evidence is incomplete and must not convert chronology into causation.

### G4 — Cross-domain reuse

Evaluate only material dependencies. Candidate links may include:
- air quality ↔ environmental health / atmospheric science;
- agriculture ↔ soil / water / food systems;
- anatomy ↔ physiology / health systems;
- antimicrobial resistance ↔ microbiology / public health;
- acid-base ↔ chemistry / analytical chemistry;
- analytical chemistry ↔ measurement science / uncertainty;
- algebra ↔ ratios / probability / measurement;
- agrifood systems ↔ agriculture / food security;
- architecture ↔ construction / building science;
- ancient civilizations ↔ archaeology / chronology / early agriculture.

No Relation is added unless it improves conceptual reuse.
