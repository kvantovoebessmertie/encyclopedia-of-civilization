# Gap Map R19 — controlled Wave 19

Date: 5 October 2026

Baseline: **438 vertical slices / 3917 records / 19 Record types / 0 blocking findings**.

## Selection basis

R19 follows the R18 CLEAN checkpoint and continues the Gap Map policy: select high-value, non-duplicate domains that increase practical usefulness and cross-domain coverage without changing the 19-type architecture.

The candidate set was reconciled against the actual `CONTENT/vertical-slices` tree. No selected R19 domain is currently present.

## Controlled ten-slice set

1. `asset-management-basics` — assets, lifecycle, performance, risk and resource planning.
2. `environmental-monitoring-basics` — observation, measurement, indicators, uncertainty and interpretation boundaries.
3. `water-resources-management-basics` — water resources, quantity, quality, distribution and contextual constraints.
4. `resource-allocation-basics` — constrained resources, competing goals, criteria and institutional context.
5. `systems-modeling-basics` — system representation, relationships, abstraction, assumptions and limitations.
6. `inspection-basics` — systematic inspection, criteria, observations, findings and professional boundaries.
7. `infrastructure-governance-basics` — institutions, long-term infrastructure decisions, accountability and lifecycle considerations.
8. `network-analysis-basics` — nodes, links, network structure, connectivity and model boundaries.
9. `geospatial-analysis-basics` — location-linked data, spatial relationships, GIS and data limitations.
10. `sampling-basics` — populations, samples, sampling design, variability and bias.

## Boundaries

The wave remains descriptive and source-bounded. It does not provide hazardous operational procedures, site-specific engineering calculations, individualized occupational/legal advice, emergency command authority, or jurisdiction-specific regulatory determinations. Context, uncertainty, professional responsibility and applicable local requirements remain explicit constraints.

## Definition of Done

R19 remains open until all ten slices pass dedicated regressions, corpus coverage and public registries are synchronized, Human View and evidence boundaries pass, package/recovery integrity passes, Reference Tests/Release Gate/Offline Edition are green, substantive R19 audit finds zero blocking findings, and the resulting CLEAN checkpoint is evidenced.

The full R1–R19 project/corpus audit remains reserved for the planned R20 gate.
