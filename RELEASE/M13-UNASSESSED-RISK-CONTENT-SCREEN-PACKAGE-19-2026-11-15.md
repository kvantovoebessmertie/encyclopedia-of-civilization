## 4. Cross-record, access and rights findings

1. `SRC-ORGANIZATIONAL_MANAGEMENT_BASICS` points to ISO Quality Management Principles. Its stored URI contains an encoded whitespace segment in the path (`%20render`) and needs URL validation. The ISO page contains an explicit AI/ML-use restriction, including prompting and generation, absent written permission. This source is on RIGHTS HOLD for this AI-assisted workflow. Candidate replacement: NIST Baldrige Excellence Framework, which describes organizational systems, management areas, process approach/deployment/learning/integration, results and measurement.
2. `SRC-PLANT_PHYSIOLOGY_BASICS` points to OpenStax Biology 2e. Its license notice restricts ingestion into LLM/generative-AI offerings without prior written permission. This source is on RIGHTS HOLD for this AI-assisted workflow. Candidate replacement: NC State Extension, “3. Botany,” Plant Physiology and Environmental Factors sections.
3. `SRC-PALEONTOLOGY_BASICS`, `SRC-PETROLOGY_BASICS`, `SRC-PLANT_PHYSIOLOGY_BASICS`, and `SRC-PROGRAMMING_LANGUAGES_BASICS` use descriptive statements as `source_identity` instead of the actual source title. `SRC-POLITICAL_ECONOMY_BASICS` and its World Bank companion use generic labels rather than exact publication/page titles. The IMF URL in `SRC-POLITICAL_ECONOMY_BASICS` also contains a misspelled author slug (`jeffrieden` rather than `jeff-frieden`); the World Bank companion's identity should be the actual page title, “Europe and Central Asia | Governance”.
4. The USGS paleontology page and the USGS petrology glossary page could not be independently retrieved by the browser in this pass. This is an access/verification limitation, not proof that the URLs are invalid. NPS fossil pages and exact USGS term pages are candidate replacements/supplements.
5. The NCBI Bookshelf histology title is not fully browseable under its publisher agreement. Do not use search snippets as if they were a reviewed full-text passage. Public NCBI MeSH entries for Histology and Clinical Pathology are better pinpoint candidates for the taxonomy claim.
6. The registered NIST/SEMATECH URL resolves to “Gallery of Distributions” (section 1.3.6.6). It is a reference for common distributions, not for the probability axioms, the 0–1 bound, or the general complement theorem.
7. The registered NIST ITL landing page is too broad for programming-language syntax and semantics; use a specific formal-language/programming-language source.
8. All Evidence Use descriptions are generic and omit exact passages. A structurally valid Claim → Evidence Use → Source chain is not, by itself, substantive evidence support.
