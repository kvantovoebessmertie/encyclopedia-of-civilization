## Consolidated findings

1. **Systemic Evidence Use locator debt:** 20/20 entries lack a complete claim-specific locator. Several source passages directly support their Claims, but that support is not preserved in the canonical Evidence Use records; E remains 1.
2. **High-consequence finding:** the FDA flood-food Claim is substantively aligned with the FDA's explicit rule and packaging-specific exceptions. Because it is safety-relevant, it remains HOLD until the exact passage, package categories, current guidance, independent food-safety review, Human View and editorial/rights gates are documented.
3. **Source-version and scope issues:** the game-theory Source points to an archived 2014 Stanford entry; use the current entry only through controlled, versioned source reconciliation. OECD's framework is explicitly an OECD “sound public governance” framework. NOAA/NGS material should retain its U.S. institutional context where relevant.
4. **Specific evidence gaps:** formal-methods Claim B needs direct model-checking support; geophysics Claim B needs method-specific support for gravity/magnetic/seismic measurements; geophysics Claim C needs direct support for the full combination of surface observations, remote measurements and models; graph-theory Claim B needs the directed-graph Chapter 6 source in addition to Chapter 5.
5. **No canonical edits:** no Claim, Evidence Use, or Source JSON was changed. No source substitution, identifier normalization, rights decision, specialist sign-off, Human View clearance, editorial approval, or publication clearance is authorized by this report.
