This supports the importance of intended intervention and context/setting, and the broad safety/effectiveness framing. WHO's separate “Management and Safe Use of Medical Devices” material says devices should be safe, of good quality, and appropriate to the settings where they are used; that gives corroborating context, but the canonical Evidence Use still links only the general “Medical Devices” page and has no exact locator.
