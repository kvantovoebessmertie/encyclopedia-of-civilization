## Passage checked

The current SAMHSA page describes disaster behavioral health as services and interventions for individuals and communities affected by disasters. It states that SAMHSA provides expertise and coordination to support partners as they prepare for, respond to, recover from, and mitigate behavioral-health effects across a range of hazards. It also lists integration of behavioral health across mitigation, preparedness, response and recovery. This directly supports the Claim's core scope.

The canonical Evidence Use is generic and does not preserve the exact page section or list locator. The claim should be read as describing a support role, not a guarantee of outcomes or a clinical intervention protocol.
