## Independence rules applied

- Different URLs from one institution or source family are not automatically independent.
- The FAO AGRIS bibliographic record is not counted as direct support for detailed process steps or parameters.
- The Met's substantive plant-fibre overview is the main accessible source for textile Claims B–D.
- FDA supports the ceramic food-contact/leaching boundary only; it is not evidence for general kiln or glaze procedure.
- A single authoritative source may be adequate for a low-risk, bounded introductory fact when a second source would add little. For safety-relevant claims, complementary sources were used where they materially improved coverage.
