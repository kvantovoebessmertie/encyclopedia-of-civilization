## Passage checked

Canonical Source: CDC/NIOSH, “About Ergonomics and Work-Related Musculoskeletal Disorders,” https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/ergonomics/about/index.html, dated February 21, 2024.

NIOSH states that ergonomics is the design of work tasks to best suit workers' capabilities (retrieved page lines 25–29). It further defines ergonomics as the design of work tasks and job demands to fit the capabilities of the working population and says the goal is to reduce and prevent work-related musculoskeletal disorders (lines 32–43). This directly supports the Claim. The canonical Evidence Use description includes a definition but does not preserve the exact locator.
