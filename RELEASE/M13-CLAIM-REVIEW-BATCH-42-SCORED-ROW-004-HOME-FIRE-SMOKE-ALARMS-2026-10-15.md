## Passage checked

U.S. Fire Administration (USFA), *Smoke Alarms*, section “Where do I put smoke alarms in my home?” The official page says to put smoke alarms inside and outside each bedroom and sleeping area, and on every level of the home; its opening statement explicitly includes the basement. The passage supports the central recommendation and the basement boundary.

Official source: https://www.usfa.fema.gov/prevention/home-fires/prepare-for-fire/smoke-alarms/index.html
