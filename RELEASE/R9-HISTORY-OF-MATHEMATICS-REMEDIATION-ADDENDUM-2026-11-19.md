## Historical and cultural safeguards

- The Oxford source explicitly cautions that modern Western categories should not automatically be projected onto historical practices in other cultures.
- The slice does not treat mathematics as a single linear transmission from one civilization or as a hierarchy of cultural achievements.
- Evidence strength and dating vary by region; disputed archaeological interpretations remain disputed.
- MacTutor's 1997 overview contains selective and value-laden comparisons; those passages are not used as support for cross-cultural claims.
- The calculus example is deliberately narrow and does not generalize one European episode to all mathematical development.
