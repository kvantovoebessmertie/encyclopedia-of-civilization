## 1. mixtures-and-solutions — 9 records

Claims:
- A: a solution is a homogeneous mixture of two or more substances.
- B: the solvent is a component of a solution, usually present at greater concentration and determining its physical state.
- C: dissolved substances are distributed in the solvent at the molecular or ionic level.

Source: OpenStax Chemistry 2e §11.1, “The Dissolution Process”  
https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/11-1-the-dissolution-process

Findings:
- §11.1 explicitly defines solutions as homogeneous mixtures of two or more substances.
- It states that the more concentrated component is called the solvent and that the physical state of the solution is typically the same as the solvent's.
- It describes solute particles as dispersed at the molecular scale and gives both molecular and ionic examples.
- The current source directly supports all three Claims.
- All three EUs nevertheless use generic descriptions without exact section/passage pinpoints.
- Context and Scope target Claim A only.
- OpenStax Chemistry 2e is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Its preface requires attribution, noncommercial use and ShareAlike for reused/adapted content and states that LLM training/ingestion requires prior written permission. Rights must be assessed against the planned release model; no textbook text, images or exercises are proposed for reuse.

Decision: **HOLD — strong direct source fit.** All three Claims are substantively supported by the current source; exact EU pinpoints, Claim-specific Context/Scope coverage and rights/reader review remain.

## 2. chemical-reactions-basics — 9 records

Claims:
- A: a chemical reaction is described as transformation of starting substances into products.
- B: a balanced chemical equation expresses quantitative relationships between reactants and products.
- C: stoichiometric coefficients connect the amounts of substances in a reaction.

Current Source: OpenStax Chemistry 2e §4.3, “Reaction Stoichiometry”  
https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/4-3-reaction-stoichiometry

Additional direct section checked:
- §4.1, “Writing and Balancing Chemical Equations”: https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/4-1-writing-and-balancing-chemical-equations

Findings:
- Claim A is directly supported by §4.1, which defines reactants as substances undergoing reaction and products as substances generated by the reaction.
- Claims B/C are directly supported by §4.1's explanation that coefficients represent relative numbers of reactant/product species and can be interpreted as ratios for any amount unit. §4.3 provides the quantitative stoichiometry framework.
- The current Source points to §4.3; it is a strong match for B/C but the best direct locator for A is §4.1.
- All three EUs are generic and contain no passage or section locators.
- Context and Scope target Claim A only.
- OpenStax Chemistry 2e rights conditions are the same CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and LLM-ingestion restrictions noted above.

Decision: **HOLD — strong substantive fit.** Use §4.1 for Claim A and §4.1/§4.3 for B/C through explicit, claim-specific EUs. Complete rights and reader review before any inclusion.
