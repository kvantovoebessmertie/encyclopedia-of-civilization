## Consolidated findings

1. **Systemic Evidence Use locator debt:** 20/20 entries lack a complete claim-specific locator. Even where a direct passage was found, the repository's Evidence Use record does not preserve it; E remains 1.
2. **Source URL/identity reconciliation:** the IEA Energy Efficiency and Energy Policy topic URLs were unavailable in this pass; the EIA Energy Markets URL was inaccessible; the World Bank Environment page is too broad for the three environmental-economics propositions; the CDC epidemiology URL was unavailable. Alternative authoritative materials were located, but they are candidates only and must not be silently substituted.
3. **Editorial/taxonomy issue:** `CLM-EPIDEMIOLOGY_BASICS-A` describes the contents of a slice rather than a domain proposition. It should be considered for editorial metadata classification, not automatic rewriting.
4. **Scope discipline:** energy-market evidence must distinguish electricity from other energy carriers; IEA energy-policy sources describe policy trade-offs and national contexts; the World Bank environmental-economics paper supports valuation/externality concepts but not every possible evaluation method; Kew's examples are geographically and culturally specific.
5. **No canonical edits:** no Claim, Evidence Use, or Source JSON was changed. No source substitution, identifier normalization, rights decision, specialist sign-off, Human View clearance, editorial approval, or publication clearance is authorized by this report.
