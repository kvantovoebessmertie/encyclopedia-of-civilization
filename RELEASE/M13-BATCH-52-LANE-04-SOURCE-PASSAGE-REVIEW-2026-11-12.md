## Consolidated findings

1. **Locator debt:** 16/20 entries lack a sufficiently specific section-level locator. Four entries (HCI-B, HCI-C, Human Systems Integration, Hydrology-A) contain useful named-section locators, but remain HOLD pending version and pinpoint reconciliation.
2. **Directly supported definitions:** heat transfer, industrial and systems engineering, infrastructure, hydrology and the WTO's basic functions have direct source matches, but their current Evidence Use records remain too generic or versionally ambiguous.
3. **Clear source mismatch:** `CLM-INFORMATION_ETHICS_BASICS-C` is not supported by the linked IFLA ethics code; the issue is evidential reliability, not library ethics. `CLM-HUMAN_COMPUTER_INTERACTION_BASICS-A` is also not directly supported by its current NIST Evidence Use passage.
4. **Other scope gaps:** industrial-engineering Claim C requires a systems-interaction source; insurance Claim C requires direct evidence on policy coverage/exclusions; labour-economics Claim B requires evidence for the employer-cost side of wages.
5. **Rights/currentness:** NIST flags its HCD page as no longer updated; MIT 6.441 Spring 2016 materials carry CC BY-NC-SA terms; IISE source is a draft PDF. These are not automatic blockers to citing the ideas, but rights/version treatment must be explicit before reuse.
6. **No canonical edits:** no Claim, Evidence Use, or Source JSON was changed. No source substitution, identifier normalization, rights decision, specialist sign-off, Human View clearance, editorial approval, or publication clearance is authorized by this report.
