## 4. Cross-record and source-quality findings

1. **NIST sampling source:** the registered URI is the NIST/SEMATECH Engineering Statistics Handbook root. It is relevant and the handbook has precise glossary and chapter locators for population, sample, representativeness, sample size and variability. Evidence Use records should cite those exact subsections rather than the handbook root alone.
2. **Scientific communication source:** the registered National Academies URI resolves to *Communicating Science Effectively: A Research Agenda*, Chapter 2, “The Complexities of Communicating Science.” This is a strong source for both Claims, but the EUs need specific paragraph/page locators.
3. **Scientific-method Source mismatch:** `SRC-SCIENTIFIC_METHOD_BASICS` has a descriptive statement as its identity, while its URI opens the front matter of *On Being a Scientist*, a responsible-conduct guide. It does not identify a precise scientific-method section. This is a confirmed identity/locator mismatch candidate.
4. **Seismology Source scope:** `SRC-SEISMOLOGY_BASICS` points to the USGS Earthquake Hazards Program hub, not the specific FAQ or science pages needed for seismographs and local shaking intensity.
5. **Sensor-system Source mismatch:** `SRC-SENSOR_SYSTEMS_BASICS` points to NIST Cybersecurity for IoT, a cybersecurity programme. It is not a technical source for sensing architecture, calibration or environmental effects.
6. **Settlement geography source:** the UN-Habitat manual is directly relevant and its stated purpose is international statistical comparison. It expressly complements rather than replaces national statistical definitions. Preserve this scope and the manual's multi-organization authorship.
7. **SI source:** NIST “SI Units” directly supports both claims. The phrase “22 derived units with special names and symbols” must not be broadened into “only 22 derived units exist.”
8. **ILO source reuse:** `SRC-SOCIAL_INSURANCE_BASICS` and `SRC-SOCIAL_PROTECTION_BASICS` point to the same ILO topic URL. They are not independent sources. The ILO flagship report is a candidate for more precise system-level and eligibility claims.
9. **Evidence Use quality:** none of the 20 EUs records a precise source passage locator. The two SI EUs are more descriptive than the generic templates elsewhere, but still need section pinpoints. Structural linkage is not a substitute for evidence detail.
10. All 20 Claims currently have `publication_status=published` and `completion_status=complete`; these fields do not override the M13 `UNASSESSED` status or constitute a fresh publication decision.
