## Safety boundary

Medical/health slices are general informational knowledge only. They must:
- avoid individualized diagnosis or treatment;
- distinguish population-level evidence from individual applicability;
- surface uncertainty and limitations;
- preserve jurisdiction/date/context where relevant;
- identify when professional or emergency services are required;
- never convert historical or descriptive material into individualized clinical instruction.

Hazard-sensitive biological content must remain descriptive and evidence-bounded.
