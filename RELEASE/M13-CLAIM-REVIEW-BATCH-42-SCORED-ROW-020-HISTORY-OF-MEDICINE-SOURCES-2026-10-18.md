## Claim under review

## Current evidence chain

The canonical Source is the National Library of Medicine's MeSH entry “History of Medicine”:
https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/mesh/68006666

The entry defines the field as the study of past events and development in medicine. It does not state that historical reconstruction uses both primary documents and interpretive literature, or that their evidentiary roles should be distinguished. The linked Evidence Use is generic and has no section-level locator.

A separate NLM Collection Development Guidelines page, “History of Medicine,” describes secondary literature as interpretive, analytical and biographical studies and discusses the role of related historical and bibliographic works:
https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK518766/

That page is relevant corroboration for the existence and role of interpretive literature, but it is a different source from the canonical MeSH entry and does not by itself fully substantiate the Claim's assertion about primary documents. It cannot be silently substituted.
