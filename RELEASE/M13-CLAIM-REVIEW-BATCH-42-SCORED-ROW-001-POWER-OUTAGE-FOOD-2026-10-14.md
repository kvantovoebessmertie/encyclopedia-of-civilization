## Passage checked

Health Canada, *Food and drinking water safety in an emergency*, section “Food safety during and after a power outage,” subsection “During a power outage”: “Do not place frozen food outside, even in winter.” The following bullets state that animals could get and contaminate the food, and that sunlight could thaw frozen food even when the outdoor temperature is very cold.

Official source: https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/food-drinking-water-safe-emergency.html  
Checked on: 2026-10-14. The page was accessible during this review; no separate publication/update date is asserted here.
