## Purpose and limits

This is a preliminary source-specific rights and attribution screen for the ten Gap Map R7 slices. It is not legal advice, a legal opinion, a permission grant or final rights clearance.

The screen separates independent factual paraphrase and citation from reproduction, translation, adaptation or redistribution of expressive source material. Public accessibility is not treated as public domain. No source text, tables, figures, images, diagrams or logos were copied into the canonical records in this audit.

All ten slices remain HOLD. The project-authored licence and distribution model are not yet adopted by the owner.

## Project-level constraints

The project-owner decision record still leaves the project-authored licence, distribution model, accountable editor/release approver and correction/takedown route unresolved. Therefore:
- no source is declared rights-cleared solely because it is publicly accessible;
- no source licence is assumed to govern project-authored Claims;
- no third-party text, translation, diagram, figure, table, image, logo or page layout may be copied into the corpus under this screen;
- source-specific attribution and licence terms must be pinned to exact versions before any first-edition inclusion;
- commercial/noncommercial compatibility must be assessed against the actual project distribution model, not an assumed one.

## Result and next action

This screen identifies constraints but does not grant permission or close the project-level rights model. All ten slices remain HOLD.
