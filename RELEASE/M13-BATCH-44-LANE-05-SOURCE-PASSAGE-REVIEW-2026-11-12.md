## Cross-cutting findings

1. **Evidence Use traceability remains the common weakness:** exact source passages are often available, but the canonical EUs do not preserve their section/paragraph/page locators. This prevents E from rising above 1 under the locked rubric.
2. **Timber engineering:** the registered USDA FPL home page is too broad for the three Claims. The 2021 Wood Handbook chapters are directly relevant: Chapter 4 moisture/physical properties, Chapter 5 mechanical properties, Chapters 7–9 stress grades/fastenings/structural analysis. The canonical Source should not be silently rewritten; versioned correction requires authorization.
3. **Volcanology:** USGS has a specific comprehensive-monitoring source listing earthquakes, ground movement, volcanic gas, chemistry and satellite analysis. The Source record currently points to the program landing page, not that specific monitoring passage.
4. **Virology:** the registered OpenStax URI is §21.1 while the strongest passage found is §21.2. This is a locator mismatch candidate, not proof the claim is false.
5. **Waste infrastructure:** EPA material directly enumerates collection, transport, storage, transfer, processing/resource recovery, treatment and disposal, but the selected canonical Source is the broad SMM page. Keep jurisdictional boundaries visible.
6. **Water quality:** the claim accurately captures U.S. EPA's three components and explicitly says standards are jurisdictional instruments. Do not generalize the U.S. legal arrangement to every country without comparative sources.
7. **Public finance and taxation:** IMF and OECD sources support the basic claims, but fiscal policy, tax policy and public financial management are related rather than interchangeable concepts.
8. **Scientific method:** OpenStax presents a useful model sequence but the process is not necessarily a rigid, universal linear algorithm; keep the introductory scope explicit.
9. **Identifier variants:** preserve stored IDs exactly; separator normalization is comparison-only.
10. **Risk flags:** all queue risk tags are triage metadata, not independent determinations of consequence or safety.
