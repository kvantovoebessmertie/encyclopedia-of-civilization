## Claim under review

> Неисправный ручной инструмент следует вывести из использования и заменить или отремонтировать до дальнейшего применения.

## Exact linked source and passage

The canonical Source points to the Canadian Centre for Occupational Health and Safety (CCOHS), “Hand Tools — General Hand Tool Operation”:

https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/hand_tools/general.html

Under “What are some basic tips when using hand tools?”, the page states:

> “Inspect tools for defects before use. Replace or repair defective tools.”

The page also advises keeping tools in good condition. Its fact sheet identifies a confirmation-current date of 2023-12-14 and last revision date of 2017-08-01.

The current Evidence Use describes the same general recommendation but has no exact passage locator. The source directly supports inspection and repair/replacement of defective tools. It does not explicitly say in this passage that a defective tool must be tagged or formally removed from service pending repair.

A separate CCOHS page for powered hand tools explicitly says a defective tool should be removed from service and tagged “Out of service,” but that is a different page and is not the currently linked Source:
https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/power_tools/saf_elec.html

That adjacent guidance is not silently substituted for the canonical evidence chain.
