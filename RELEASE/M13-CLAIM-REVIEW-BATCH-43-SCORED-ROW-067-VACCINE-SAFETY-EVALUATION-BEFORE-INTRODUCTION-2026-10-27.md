## Passage checked

The canonical Source is WHO, “Vaccines and immunization: Vaccine safety,” updated 23 September 2025. WHO states that before a vaccine is introduced in a country, it undergoes rigorous testing through multiple phases of clinical trials and health authorities evaluate the results for safety and efficacy. The page describes laboratory tests and clinical trial phases I–III, and also notes that safety monitoring continues after introduction. This directly supports the Claim's core point. The canonical Evidence Use has no exact passage locator; the Claim must not imply that pre-introduction trials replace ongoing safety monitoring.
