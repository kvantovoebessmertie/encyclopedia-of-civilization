## Cross-cutting results

- 30/30 Claim files were opened. All inspected Claims have `record_type: claim`; filename/internal-ID comparison is exact for 27, while the three Data Governance Claims use underscore-form internal IDs against hyphenated filenames. No identifier was rewritten.
- The three Data Governance Evidence Use IDs and Source ID also use underscore forms, and their Claim/Source refs resolve under separator-only comparison. This is an alias/reconciliation finding, not a broken reference and not authorization to rename records.
- Primary EUs use `supports` and resolve to the expected Claim and Source. Critical Thinking A/B/C have additional ACRL Evidence Use records; cybersecurity Claim C has an additional CISA EU; source independence is **not** established by distinct organization names alone.
- Multiple source identities are descriptive or too broad for their stated purpose: cryptography's Source identity is a sentence; cybersecurity's Source identity is a description rather than the NIST CSF title; Data Governance points to the NIST ITL landing page; Cultural Heritage uses a broad query-list URL.
- Methodological/editorial candidates: `CLM-CRITICAL_THINKING_BASICS-A/B/C/D` (review as a group), `CLM-CULTURAL_ANTHROPOLOGY-C`, `CLM-CULTURE_AND_LANGUAGE-C`, `CLM-DATA_ETHICS_BASICS-C`, and `CLM-DATA_SCIENCE_BASICS-A/B/C`.
- Security-relevant candidates requiring additional review: all four cybersecurity Claims in this batch, especially the CISA baseline-practices Claim. No operational procedure or content edit was authorized by this triage.
