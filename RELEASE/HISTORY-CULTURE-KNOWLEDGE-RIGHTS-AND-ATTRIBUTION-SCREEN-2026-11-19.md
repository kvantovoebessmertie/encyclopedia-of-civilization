Preliminary source-specific rights screen for the six Gap Map R8 history/culture slices. This is not legal advice, a permission grant or final rights clearance.

## Project-level constraints

The project-owner decision record still leaves the project-authored licence, distribution model, accountable editor/release approver and correction/takedown route unresolved. Therefore:
- no source is declared rights-cleared solely because it is accessible online;
- item-level rights must be checked for every map, archive, manuscript, artifact, image or digitized primary source;
- no third-party text, translation, scan, image, map, diagram or layout may be copied into the corpus under this screen;
- any cultural/ethnographic field material requires consent, privacy, confidentiality and community-sensitivity review in addition to copyright review;
- factual paraphrase and bibliographic citation still require exact attribution and source accuracy.
