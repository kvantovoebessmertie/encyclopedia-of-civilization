## Claim under review

> Продовольственная безопасность на уровне домохозяйства означает доступ всех его членов в любое время к достаточному количеству пищи для активной и здоровой жизни.

## Passage checked

Canonical Source: USDA Economic Research Service, “Food Security in the U.S. — Measurement,” https://ers.usda.gov/topics/food-nutrition-assistance/food-security-in-the-us/measurement, updated March 30, 2026.

The page defines household food security as “access by all members at all times to enough food for an active, healthy life” and lists availability of nutritionally adequate and safe foods and the ability to acquire acceptable foods as minimum elements (retrieved page lines 103–109). This directly supports the Claim's definition. The canonical Evidence Use does not preserve the “What Is Food Security?” heading as a pinpoint.
