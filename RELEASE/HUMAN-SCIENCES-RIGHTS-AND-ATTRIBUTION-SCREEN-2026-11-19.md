## Purpose and limits

This screen reviews the exact source versions used by the remediated `psychology-basics`, `neuroscience-basics` and `developmental-biology-basics` slices. It records public copyright/licence/permission evidence and the attribution route.

This is a preliminary source-specific screen, not legal advice, permission, final rights clearance, or editorial approval. It does not determine the law in every publication jurisdiction. The current canonical records contain independently worded factual propositions and pinpoint citations; the remediation did not copy source paragraphs, tables, figures, diagrams, images, scales, tests or other expressive source material.

Before any of these slices can be considered for INCLUDE:
- attach the exact attribution to the reader-facing source route;
- obtain a named human rights review and record reviewer/date;
- resolve any permission or project-use question under the source-specific terms;
- complete independent editorial/Human View review of the rendered reader presentation;
- preserve the explicit non-clinical boundary for psychology/neuroscience and the animal-development boundary for developmental biology.
