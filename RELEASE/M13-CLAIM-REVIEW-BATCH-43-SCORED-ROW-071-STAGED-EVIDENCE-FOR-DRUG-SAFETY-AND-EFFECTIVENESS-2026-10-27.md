## Claim under review

## Passage checked

The FDA overview describes staged drug development: preclinical laboratory/animal research, clinical research in people, regulatory review and post-market safety monitoring. FDA's detailed clinical-research material explains that trials are designed around specific questions, selection criteria and study populations, and that phases have different purposes. This supports the Claim's main point that evidence accumulates in stages and the roles of preclinical and clinical data differ. The current canonical Source is the broad process page and the Evidence Use lacks an exact locator for the population/indication limitation.
