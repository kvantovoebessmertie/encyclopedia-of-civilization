## Finding

The Package 31 results table lists four Claims as direct conceptual support:
- CLM-MAPS_AND_NAVIGATION_BASICS-A
- CLM-MAPS_AND_NAVIGATION_BASICS-B
- CLM-RISK_MANAGEMENT_BASICS-C (COSO only; ISO 31000 excluded)
- CLM-STATISTICS_BASICS-B

The table lists eight Claims as partial or rights hold:
- CLM-MANUFACTURING_SYSTEMS_BASICS-B
- CLM-MANUFACTURING_SYSTEMS_BASICS-C
- CLM-MAPS_AND_NAVIGATION_BASICS-C
- CLM-MATERIALS_BASICS-A
- CLM-MATERIALS_BASICS-C
- CLM-M5-MATERIALS-PERFORMANCE-D
- CLM-MATERIALS_SCIENCE_BASICS-B
- CLM-RISK_MANAGEMENT_BASICS-B

Therefore the summary and decision-section counts “3 direct / 9 partial” are inconsistent with the 12 row-level dispositions. The row-level table reconciles to **4 direct conceptual support / 8 partial or rights hold**.

## Scope and controls

This erratum corrects only the arithmetic summary of Package 31. It does not change any row-level finding, canonical Claim/Evidence Use/Source record, D/E/B value, rights status, publication status or M13 release decision.

“Direct conceptual support” remains a qualitative screening outcome only. The four Claims remain UNASSESSED and subject to exact locator, scope, rights, specialist and Human View gates as applicable. Risk Management C is supported by COSO only; ISO 31000 remains excluded under the existing rights hold.

No canonical writes are authorized or performed.
