## Audit objective

Identify genuine remaining subject-matter and cross-domain gaps before defining P13. The audit explicitly rejects expansion by quota, duplicate domains, and artificial Relations.

## Findings

### A. Genuine system-level gap candidate

**Human settlements / settlement systems — HIGH priority**

Existing coverage includes settlement geography, urbanization, urban economics, urban planning, housing systems, infrastructure, transportation, water, energy, waste and public works. These components are individually represented, but the corpus lacks a dedicated bounded system-level layer describing how a human settlement functions as an interconnected system.

Decision: **candidate for new vertical slice**, subject to final substantive scope design.

### B. Existing domains requiring integration rather than duplicate slices

1. **Healthcare / care delivery — integration / targeted deepening**
   Existing health-systems, public-health, epidemiology, medical-device, biomedical and related slices cover major components. A new generic medical-system slice is not justified yet.

2. **Agricultural production chain — integration / targeted deepening**
   Agriculture, agricultural engineering, agrifood systems, soil, water, livestock, food systems and distribution already exist. Priority is to connect the chain rather than duplicate it.

3. **Industrial systems — integration / targeted deepening**
   Manufacturing, manufacturing processes/systems, industrial engineering and industrial safety already cover the major components. A generic industrial-systems slice is not yet justified.

4. **Built environment — cross-domain integration**
   Housing, construction, building science/envelope/services, energy, water, waste, accessibility and land-use planning already exist. The principal gap is integration.

### C. Structural remaining gap

**Cross-domain linkage remains incomplete.**

Relations should be added only where a dependency materially improves reuse and can be expressed without asserting causality or truth.

High-value candidate linkage clusters:
- settlement ↔ housing ↔ infrastructure;
- settlement ↔ transport ↔ energy ↔ water ↔ waste;
- settlement ↔ public health/environmental health;
- agriculture ↔ water ↔ soil ↔ food systems ↔ distribution;
- healthcare ↔ public health ↔ health systems;
- industry ↔ manufacturing ↔ energy ↔ logistics ↔ waste.

## Explicitly rejected as standalone P13 topics

The audit does not justify new generic slices merely for:
- transportation systems;
- medical systems;
- industrial systems;
- agricultural systems;
- economic systems;
- generic technology systems.

These areas already have substantial component coverage.

## P13 design principle

P13 is **not a fixed ten-slice expansion**.

The preferred structure is:

**1 genuinely missing system-level vertical + targeted maturation of existing slices + justified cross-domain Relations + full evidence/Human View audit.**

The final number of changes is determined by the audit, not by quota.

## Epistemic boundary

This audit identifies structural/content gaps; it does not establish the factual truth of external Claims.
