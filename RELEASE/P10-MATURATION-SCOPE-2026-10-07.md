It also creates strong potential links to economics, history, civilization, infrastructure, migration, and public-health knowledge already in the corpus.

## Safety and applicability boundary

Political and legal material must remain descriptive, jurisdiction- and date-bounded, and non-electoral.

P10 must not provide individualized legal advice, individualized tax advice, or instructions intended to evade law, regulation, sanctions, or institutional controls.

Economic and social claims must distinguish descriptive evidence from normative judgments and must not silently convert correlation or temporal sequence into causation.

Migration and mobility content must distinguish general concepts from jurisdiction-specific eligibility, procedures, deadlines, and rights.

Historical material must preserve temporal scope, source role, uncertainty, and contested interpretations where applicable.
