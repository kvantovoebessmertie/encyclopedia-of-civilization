Date: 2026-11-19

## Purpose and limits

This is a preliminary source-specific rights and attribution screen for the ten social-systems/public-institutions slices. It is not legal advice, a legal opinion, a permission grant or final rights clearance.

The screen distinguishes:
- factual paraphrase and bibliographic citation in the current records;
- copying, excerpting, translating, adapting or redistributing expressive source material;
- item-specific third-party rights from the project-owner's still-unresolved licence and distribution decisions.

No source paragraphs, figures, tables, images, logos or extended expressive passages were copied into the current canonical records. Public accessibility is not treated as public domain or blanket permission. All ten slices remain HOLD.

## Project-level constraints

The first-edition owner decision record still leaves the project-authored licence, distribution model, accountable editor/release approver and correction/takedown contact unresolved. Therefore:
- no source is declared rights-cleared merely because it is public;
- no CC licence is inferred for project records from a source's own licence;
- no source text, translation, image, table, chart or logo may be copied into the corpus under this screen;
- factual paraphrase still requires source accuracy, attribution and review of applicable law and terms;
- any use of a source's expressive material must be checked against both its licence and the final project distribution model.
