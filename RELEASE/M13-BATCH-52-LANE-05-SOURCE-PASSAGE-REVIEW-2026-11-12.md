## Consolidated findings

1. **Evidence Use locator debt:** all 20 entries lack a fully reproducible claim-specific pinpoint in the canonical Evidence Use record. NIMH entries contain the strongest named-section locators; NASA RCM links to Chapter 7 but omits the relevant subparagraphs.
2. **Direct source matches:** BLS CPS, NIMH Learning and Memory, NASA RCM, UNIDO Smart Manufacturing, NIST Manufacturing Operations, USGS map/compass, NIST materials-science division and NSF materials research all contain material relevant to the corresponding Claims.
3. **Important source gaps:** labour-market Claim C's list extends beyond the ILO wage page; labour-law Claim B needs a direct explanation of international standards and national implementation; map Claim C needs support for currentness/field conditions; materials Claim C/D need direct application-specific performance and failure evidence.
4. **Version/authority boundaries:** NASA NPR 8831.2F is an agency directive and its applicability should remain bounded to the stated NASA context; the World Bank logistics PDF requires a title/date/page locator; current NIST division pages should replace the old landing page only through controlled source reconciliation.
5. **No canonical edits:** no Claim, Evidence Use, or Source JSON was changed. No source substitution, identifier normalization, rights decision, specialist sign-off, Human View clearance, editorial approval, or publication clearance is authorized by this report.
