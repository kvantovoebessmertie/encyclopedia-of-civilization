## 1. Microeconomics — 1 Claim

Canonical Source: OpenStax Principles of Economics 3e.

### CLM-MICROECONOMICS_BASICS-A
Claim: “Demand and supply describe the relationship between price and quantity under given conditions.”

Exact support:
- OpenStax §3.1 defines demand as the amount consumers are willing and able to purchase at each price and supply as the amount producers are willing to produce/bring to market at each price.
- It describes demand and supply schedules/curves as representations of the price-quantity relationships.
- The section explicitly states that the law of demand and law of supply assume other variables affecting demand or supply are held constant; §3.2 explains the ceteris paribus assumption and factors that shift the curves.

Assessment: DIRECT support. Proposed locator: OpenStax Principles of Economics 3e §3.1, “Demand for Goods and Services”, “Supply of Goods and Services” and “Equilibrium”; §3.2, “Shifts in Demand and Supply”.

## 2. Accounting — 3 Claims

Canonical Source: IFRS Foundation, Conceptual Framework for Financial Reporting (revised 2018).

### CLM-ACCOUNTING_BASICS-A
Claim: “Accounting generates information about business transactions and financial position.”

Exact support:
- The Conceptual Framework sets out the objective of general-purpose financial reporting and describes the reporting entity and its boundary.
- It defines assets, liabilities, equity, income and expenses and explains recognition, derecognition, measurement, presentation and disclosure.

Assessment: PARTIAL. The framework directly supports generation of financial information about financial position and performance, but the current source page does not establish the full bookkeeping/recording process for every business transaction. Add a primary accounting-textbook or accounting-standard passage on recording transactions and the accounting cycle if the first half of the Claim is intended literally. Proposed locator: IFRS Conceptual Framework, Chapters 1–5 (objective, reporting entity, elements, recognition and measurement).

### CLM-ACCOUNTING_BASICS-B
Claim: “Financial statements present information in a structured form for users of financial statements.”

Exact support:
- The IFRS Conceptual Framework describes the objective of general-purpose financial reporting and identifies presentation and disclosure as part of the framework.
- It states that financial statements provide information about assets, liabilities, equity, income and expenses that is useful to existing and potential investors, lenders and other creditors.

Assessment: DIRECT support. Proposed locator: IFRS Conceptual Framework, Chapter 1 “Objective of General Purpose Financial Reporting” and Chapter 7 “Presentation and Disclosure”.

### CLM-ACCOUNTING_BASICS-C
Claim: “Accounting estimates and classifications depend on the standards applied and the circumstances.”

Exact support:
- The IFRS Conceptual Framework defines recognition criteria and measurement bases and provides guidance on when they are used.
- It describes the reporting entity and the circumstances and information relevant to measurement, presentation and disclosure.

Assessment: DIRECT at the conceptual level. Preserve the boundary that specific classifications and estimates depend on the applicable accounting framework, transaction facts and relevant standard; the general framework is not a substitute for a particular IFRS/GAAP requirement. Proposed locator: IFRS Conceptual Framework, Chapters 4–6 and 7.

## 3. Administrative law — 1 Claim

Canonical Source: Cornell Law School Legal Information Institute, Wex, “Administrative law”.

### CLM-ADMINISTRATIVE_LAW_BASICS-A
Claim: “Administrative law regulates the creation and activity of administrative bodies.”

Exact support:
- Cornell Wex defines administrative law as the branch of law governing the creation and operation of administrative agencies.
- It identifies agency powers, substantive rules, and legal relationships between agencies, other governmental bodies and the public.
- The page also describes agencies' statutory basis and their roles in interpreting, administering and enforcing laws.

Assessment: DIRECT as a general introductory description of the U.S. administrative-law framework. The source is explicitly U.S.-oriented; do not present its agency structure or procedures as universal. Add a comparative or jurisdiction-specific source before applying the Claim to another legal system. Proposed locator: Cornell Wex, “Administrative law”, opening paragraph and “Structure of Agencies”.

## 4. Behavioral economics — 1 Claim

Canonical Source: Nobel Prize, 2002 Prize in Economic Sciences press release.

### CLM-BEHAVIORAL_ECONOMICS_BASICS-A
Claim: “Behavioral economics integrates research on human behavior into economic analysis.”

Exact support:
- The 2002 Nobel Prize press release says Daniel Kahneman integrated insights from psychological research into economic science, especially human judgment and decision-making under uncertainty.
- It describes Kahneman and Amos Tversky's prospect theory as an alternative to standard economic theory that better accounts for observed behavior.
- The release describes psychological research on judgment and decision-making and experimental testing of economic assumptions as foundations of psychological and experimental economics.

Assessment: DIRECT support for the stated disciplinary integration. The Nobel citation is a concise historical source; add a current textbook or survey if the Claim is intended as a full definition of the contemporary field rather than a foundational statement. Proposed locator: Nobel Prize 2002 press release, “Psychological and experimental economics” and the paragraph on Kahneman's contributions.

## 5. Cell biology — 3 Claims

Canonical Source: OpenStax Biology 2e, Chapter 4, “Cell Structure”.

### CLM-CELL_BASICS-A
Claim: “The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of living organisms.”

Exact support:
- OpenStax §4.1 states that cells are the basic building blocks of organisms and presents the unified cell theory: one or more cells comprise all living things, the cell is the basic unit of life, and new cells arise from existing cells.

Assessment: DIRECT support. Proposed locator: OpenStax Biology 2e §4.1, “Cell Theory”.

### CLM-CELL_BASICS-B
Claim: “Prokaryotic cells do not have a membrane-bound nucleus, whereas eukaryotic cells do.”

Exact support:
- OpenStax §4.2 describes prokaryotic cells and states that their DNA is not membrane-bound.
- §4.3 states that eukaryotic cells have a membrane-bound nucleus and other membrane-bound organelles.

Assessment: DIRECT support. Proposed locator: OpenStax Biology 2e §§4.2 “Prokaryotic Cells” and 4.3 “Eukaryotic Cells”.

### CLM-CELL_BASICS-C
Claim: “Cell structures perform different functions within the cell.”

Exact support:
- OpenStax §4.3 describes the functions of the nucleus, endoplasmic reticulum, Golgi apparatus, chloroplasts, mitochondria and other organelles.
- §4.2 describes shared components such as the plasma membrane, cytoplasm, DNA and ribosomes and their roles.

Assessment: DIRECT support. Proposed locator: OpenStax Biology 2e §§4.2–4.3, organelle/component descriptions and summary.

## 6. Civil society — 1 Claim

Canonical Source: World Bank, public reference document on civil society.

### CLM-CIVIL_SOCIETY_BASICS-A
Claim: “Civil society generally describes a space of voluntary associations distinct from the state and the commercial sector.”

Exact support checked:
- The canonical World Bank PDF URI was identified in the Source record but could not be fully retrieved in this pass because the PDF exceeded the available retrieval size.
- An independently accessible World Bank document describes civil society as a public sphere in which citizens and voluntary organizations engage, distinct from the state and market, while noting that boundaries can be difficult to establish.
- OECD DAC Recommendation on Enabling Civil Society states that civil society is uncoerced human association or interaction addressing shared needs, ideas, interests, values, faith and beliefs, and that it is distinct from states, private for-profit enterprises and the family.

Assessment: PARTIAL for the current canonical source chain; the concept is supported by authoritative corroborating sources, but the canonical World Bank PDF's exact passage was not verified. Add the exact OECD DAC definition as a separately identified Source/Evidence Use or retrieve the canonical PDF and record its page/section. Preserve “generally” because boundaries between civil society, state and market are not always clear.
