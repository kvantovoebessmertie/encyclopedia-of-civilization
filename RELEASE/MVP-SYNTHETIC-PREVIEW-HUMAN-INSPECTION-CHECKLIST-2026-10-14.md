## Decision rule

- Any FAIL blocks acceptance until fixed and re-inspected on a new exact commit.
- Any BLOCKED or NOT RUN item means human inspection is incomplete.
- A successful CI run does not replace this inspection.
- Passing this synthetic fixture does not qualify any real Claim or source for publication.
- Real-record pilot selection still requires exact ID/version allowlisting, source and rights checks, editorial approval, correction/takedown and privacy/retention policies, an accountable owner, and a separate release gate.
