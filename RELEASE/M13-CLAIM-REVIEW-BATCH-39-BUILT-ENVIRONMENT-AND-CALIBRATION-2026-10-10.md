## Method

## Consolidated findings

1. **Confirmed generic Evidence Use debt:** all 15 reviewed Evidence Use records use non-specific formulations such as “source is used within the stated material/scope” or “source supports the claim within the stated scope.” They do not identify a passage, section, table, definition or subclaim.
2. **Evidence fit is stronger at topic level than at claim level:** FHWA's landing page directly supports the broad bridge-connectivity and activity-list claims. NIST Building Science, CIBSE Building Services and NIST Calibration Services are relevant source identities, but their landing-page locators do not establish every detailed proposition in the associated claims.
3. **Practical depth gaps:** the built-environment slices lack worked examples that connect observed conditions to mechanisms, measurements, failure modes and a qualified conclusion. The calibration slice lacks a clear example linking the measurement requirement, instrument range/uncertainty, calibration result, traceability and acceptance decision.
4. **High-consequence boundary:** bridge condition, building-envelope moisture, ventilation, electrical/water systems and calibration quality can affect safety. These introductory Claims are not engineering instructions, structural assessments, building-code determinations, commissioning plans or calibration certificates. Keep qualified-person, local-code and object-specific boundaries explicit.
5. **Source independence is not demonstrated by multiple links here:** each of these Claims has one linked Source record. A relevant institutional source is useful, but it is not independent corroboration by itself.
6. No content Records were changed. This batch records audit findings only; it does not authorize content edits.
