## 2. Macroeconomics — direct employment locator and two wording corrections

The IMF article **“Micro and Macro: The Economic Divide”** directly states that macroeconomics studies employment, GDP and inflation and focuses on aggregates such as national income, savings and the overall price level:

https://www.imf.org/en/Publications/fandd/issues/Series/Back-to-Basics/micro-and-macro

This is a direct source for Claim A's employment/GDP/inflation scope. The parent report's proposed locator set can therefore use this article alongside the IMF GDP and Inflation articles. The series page alone is not a substitute for the exact article-level locator.

**Claim B — “market value” is too narrow.** The IMF GDP article says GDP measures the *monetary value* of final goods and services produced in a country during a period. It includes some non-market government production, such as defense and education services. Recommended wording:

> ВВП измеряет денежную стоимость конечных товаров и услуг, произведённых внутри страны за определённый период.

Direct locator: IMF, “Gross Domestic Product: An Economy’s All” → “Measuring GDP”:
https://www.imf.org/en/Publications/fandd/issues/Series/Back-to-Basics/gross-domestic-product-gdp

**Claim C — “sustained” is not needed for the IMF's direct definition.** The IMF article defines inflation as the rate of increase in prices over a given period, most commonly measured over a year. Recommended wording:

> Инфляция — это темп роста общего уровня цен за определённый период времени.

Direct locator: IMF, “Inflation: Prices on the Rise” → “What is inflation and how it is measured”:
https://www.imf.org/en/publications/fandd/issues/series/back-to-basics/inflation

Do not equate inflation with a one-time price increase in a single good. Keep the chosen price index and its coverage explicit.

## 3. Microeconomics — retain the existing book, correct the unsupported clause

The parent Batch 12 source, OpenStax *Principles of Economics 3e*, is already suitable for the demand/supply/equilibrium Claims. It covers both microeconomic and macroeconomic material and does not need to be replaced merely to use the microeconomics-specific sibling title.

- §3.1 directly defines demand, supply, equilibrium price and equilibrium quantity.
- §3.2 explains shifts in demand and supply and the ceteris paribus assumption.
- §3.5 adds the relationship between demand, supply and market efficiency.

Sources:
- §3.1: https://openstax.org/books/principles-economics-3e/pages/3-1-demand-supply-and-equilibrium-in-markets-for-goods-and-services
- §3.2: https://openstax.org/books/principles-economics-3e/pages/3-2-shifts-in-demand-and-supply-for-goods-and-services
- §3.5: https://openstax.org/books/principles-economics-3e/pages/3-5-demand-supply-and-efficiency

Claim C's phrase “and the chosen market definition” is not established as a separate general rule by these sections. Recommended narrowing:

> Рыночное равновесие достигается при цене, при которой количество товара, которое покупатели готовы приобрести, равно количеству, которое продавцы готовы предложить.

**Critical rights correction:** the current OpenStax *Principles of Economics 3e* preface identifies CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, not CC BY 4.0, and explicitly states that the book may not be used to train or otherwise be ingested into LLMs or generative-AI offerings without prior written permission. It also restricts commercial reuse and requires attribution and ShareAlike for permitted adaptations.

Official preface: https://openstax.org/books/principles-economics-3e/pages/preface

The parent Batch 12 statement that this book is CC BY 4.0 is incorrect and must be corrected. This is a material project-level rights blocker. Do not use the textbook as a cleared source until the project obtains the required written permission or selects a rights-compatible source.

## 5. Organizational Behavior — exact book identity and rights blocker

The OpenStax *Organizational Behavior* textbook is a more direct educational source than the current generic APA organizational-psychology topic page for the existing Claims:

- §1.4 defines organizational behavior and distinguishes individual, group and organization levels.
- §7.1 covers motivation.
- §11.1 covers managerial/interpersonal communication.
- §16.1 covers organizational structure and design.
- §9.2 covers work-group structure, roles and norms.

Sources:
- §1.4: https://openstax.org/books/organizational-behavior/pages/1-4-a-model-of-organizational-behavior-and-management
- §7.1: https://openstax.org/books/organizational-behavior/pages/7-1-motivation-direction-and-intensity

**Claim C boundary:** §1.4 supports levels of analysis and organizational context, but it does not establish a general research-method rule about observation conditions. Narrow the Claim to levels of analysis and organizational context, or add a distinct source on organizational research design, sampling and observation methods.

**Critical rights finding:** the OpenStax *Organizational Behavior* preface states CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and explicitly prohibits LLM training/ingestion and use in generative-AI offerings without prior written permission. Commercial reuse is not granted. This is a material rights blocker, not a cleared source.

Official preface: https://openstax.org/books/organizational-behavior/pages/preface

Do not change the canonical APA Source to OpenStax without an owner/legal decision. A rights-compatible alternative may be preferable. No new Source/EU ID is invented here.
