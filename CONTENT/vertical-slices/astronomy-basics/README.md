## NASA evidence pinpoints

- `CLM-ASTRONOMY_BASICS-A`: NASA Science, *Chapter 1: The Solar System*, “Knowledge of the Solar System” and “Mass” — the Sun, objects orbiting it and the Sun’s dominant gravitational influence.
- `CLM-ASTRONOMY_BASICS-C`: the same chapter, “Distances Within the Solar System” and “Light Time” — astronomical unit and light-year/light-time scales.

Claim B remains PARTIAL/HOLD: the current NASA chapter does not establish all physical and orbital distinctions among planets, dwarf planets, asteroids and comets. Separate NASA/IAU classification and orbital-property evidence is still required. NASA page-level third-party credits and rights remain a separate review.
