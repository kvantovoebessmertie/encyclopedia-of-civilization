# public-health-surveillance-basics

Matured P6 slice. Source → 4 Claims → 4 Evidence Use → Context → Scope.

Evidence is independently triangulated with WHO and CDC sources. Surveillance signals require contextual interpretation and do not constitute individual diagnosis or live public-health directives.

Human View boundary: surveillance data must be presented as signals with time, population, geography and methodology where relevant. A signal must not be silently converted into an individual diagnosis or current public-health instruction; urgent situations require current authoritative guidance and appropriate professional/emergency escalation.
