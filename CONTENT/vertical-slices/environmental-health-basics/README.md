# environmental-health-basics

Matured P6 slice. Source → 4 Claims → 4 Evidence Use → Context → Scope.

Evidence is independently triangulated with WHO and US EPA sources. Exposure pathways are descriptive; individual risk and causality require context and appropriate evidence.

Human View boundary: environmental-health information must preserve exposure, population, time, geography and method. It must not be rendered as an individualized risk diagnosis or emergency exposure-treatment instruction without current case-specific evidence and appropriate professional/emergency guidance.
